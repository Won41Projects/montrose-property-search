# Naturita town hall

Uncompahgre Medical Center is hosting. The room is theirs. Thank them, draw one line, ask one question, and stop.

UMC is a weekday clinic in Norwood. It is not a hospital. Norwood is not in the West End mill. Basin Clinic is inside the district, and the hospital takes Basin’s place. UMC stays.

## Say this

I’m glad you’re here. A regular doctor, a dentist, a pharmacy, and a ride to Norwood help people while they are stable. None of that is an emergency room at 2 in the morning, a bed overnight, or a place to recover here instead of in Montrose.

From the West End, Montrose or Grand Junction is close to 2 hours. Norwood is 20 to 30 minutes, on a road that usually stays ordinary. The roads to Montrose and Grand Junction do not. At night or in weather, those trips can be treacherous. A clinic still cannot keep someone overnight. A hospital in Nucla puts the bed inside that shorter, steadier drive, and it does not replace the clinic in Norwood.

## If they lean on aging in place

Primary care and a pharmacy help people stay home while they are well. Home health and assisted living are a gap their own CEO has named, not a program the clinic runs. A house call from an ambulance crew still cannot keep someone overnight. The Norwood fire district is training paramedics for home visits. That is useful, and it is not a bed. Aging in place fails at the first illness that needs one. That bed is in Montrose now.

## Ask one question, then stop

1. Which service on this flyer is open at 2 a.m. on a Sunday?
2. When someone in Naturita has chest pain tonight, what is open, and what can UMC bill for that trip?
3. Is UMC asking this room to vote no on 6A and 6B?

## If they walk the flyer

Carina is the existing network of Colorado community health centers. UMC is already in it. The new rural-health grant went to that network, not to a hospital.

Chronic-disease work is the clinic’s current quality program. It is office care.

There is no public record that UMC runs TeleROOTS. As published, that is telehealth for addiction treatment, built for the Eastern Plains. It is not an emergency room.

Behavioral health at UMC is real counseling. It is not a hospital unit.

The shuttle is real. It is a ride to a UMC appointment, not a ride to a bed.

## Leave alone

Do not call the meeting illegal. Do not say “critical care.” Do not argue the levy, the spreadsheet, or Norwood joining the district. Do not claim the biggest dollars in the West End estimate are the ones a clinic cannot bill. Pharmacy and primary care are the larger share. The emergency, the stay, the imaging, and the therapy are the share a clinic cannot keep.
