# Meeting with Chuck Porth

Tuesday, 4:00 p.m. Your office, 140 East Main Street, Naturita, first floor. Plan on 30 minutes.

He asked for this. Let him give UMC’s take before you give yours. Write down the claim he actually makes.

In June he did not say he opposes a hospital. He told the commissioners UMC is not in favor of the service plan as written, because it would fund medicine, dentistry, mental health, and an outpatient pharmacy, “all things that would directly compete with us.” He said the two clinics should have built the plan together, and he told the county to look at the whole area, including San Miguel County.

## If he cites the 40 percent

Agree. In 2025, UMC saw 644 patients from the Nucla and Naturita ZIP codes, about 30 percent of its whole panel, and about 4 in 10 residents of those ZIP codes. That is office care, and it is real.

## Then say this

The hospital replaces Basin Clinic. It does not replace UMC. Basin is inside the district. You are in Norwood, outside the mill and outside the plan.

What you bill today is the visit, the dental care, the counseling, and the prescription. What you cannot bill is the night, the emergency, and the bed. Those patients leave for Montrose or Grand Junction. From Norwood, Montrose is about 90 minutes. From the West End, Montrose or Grand Junction is close to 2 hours. The West End is 20 to 30 minutes from you, on a road that usually stays ordinary. The roads out to Montrose and Grand Junction can be treacherous at night or in weather.

A hospital in Nucla gives you a place to send the patient you cannot keep, and a reason for that patient to come back to you.

## The offer, then stop

You told the commissioners the plan competes with you on medicine, dentistry, mental health, and pharmacy. Dentistry, behavioral health, and your pharmacy should stay yours. The hospital replaces Basin’s daytime practice inside the district. It should not try to become Norwood’s medical home.

You keep primary care, dental, behavioral health, and your pharmacy. The hospital keeps the emergency room, the stay, the imaging, and the swing bed, and it sends people back. What would that split need to look like for UMC?

## Only if he asks for numbers

UMC is about a $6.4 million clinic. About $4.2 million of that is care, and about $2.2 million is grants. Basin Clinic collects under half a million dollars a year in patient revenue, and that clinic goes away when the hospital opens.

Of the West End estimate, pharmacy is about $2.9 million and primary care about $1.4 million. A clinic can already touch those. Emergency care, stays, imaging, and therapy are about $3.8 million. That is the part neither clinic can bill.

Do not say the biggest tickets are the ones a clinic cannot capture. Pharmacy is the largest line.

## Only if he says the projections are unrealistic

A draft page still posted on the Basin Clinic website added the insurance write-offs instead of subtracting them. The plan the commissioners approved already corrects that line. The website file is the old copy. Then stop.

## Optional closer

Use this only if the meeting is still friendly. Skip it if the room is tense. Do not put a dollar figure on it, and do not say Norwood is getting a free ride.

You’re right that this was built from one side of the county line. I’m not asking Norwood to pay this mill. What we are building stops at San Miguel County. If your people are using the hospital for the things a clinic cannot do, there may come a day when Norwood wants a vote and a seat instead of being a guest. That would be Norwood’s decision, and San Miguel County’s process, not mine. I would welcome that conversation when the hospital is real. Until then, the win is simpler. UMC keeps the medical home. The hospital takes the night, the emergency, and the bed, and sends people back to you.

## Leave alone unless he opens the door

The levy math, Rangely’s tax bill, and whether the town hall was fair. If he raises Rangely, a hospital that size can hold on the order of $19 million in local care, and patient revenue alone did not pay its bills. Do not use Rangely as a forecast for Nucla.
